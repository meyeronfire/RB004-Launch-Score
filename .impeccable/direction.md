# Direction Contract

## Authority

The user's approved master prompt fixes the identity and page architecture. Apply it directly; do not replace it with a concept tournament. Code-first implementation is used in this session because the user's orchestration selects Luna; no standing workflow preference is inferred.

## Modes

Landing: Persuade. Scanning and report: Operate. Legal and setup documentation: Read.

## First Viewport

An unframed black editorial canvas. Regular Basis and RB004 provide a compact catalog header. A large, tightly set left-aligned question anchors the page: “Will Your Launch Page Convert a Stranger?” The primary underline URL field and hot pink submit action appear above the fold at 1280×720. The visitor immediately understands the URL-to-report interaction. Whitespace and type establish hierarchy; no decorative card containers.

## Signature Interaction

The submitted URL yields a truthful live step log followed by a monumental metallic numeric score with a restrained pink marker and minimally drawn category bars. Reduced motion bypasses animation. Unavailable checks remain visible rather than turning into fabricated readings.

## System

Black #000, white #fff, hot pink #FF2E7E, hover #E0186A, pink tint rgba(255,46,126,.14); secondary text at AA contrast. Inter display/body, Geist Mono for measurements and labels. The prompt explicitly permits metallic gradient text for the score and numbered 01–04 editorial checks. No other hues. No outlined card grids, inflated radii, decorative icon tiles, invented proof, or background gradient blobs.

## Visitor Path

Submit public URL → real scan stages → free score, category readings, screenshots and top three issues → optional verified checkout → full evidence, copy and tool-specific prompt. Example report is labeled. Supporting editorial checks, two-column pricing, FAQ, plain legal pages, and verified studio catalog links complete the landing.

## Finish Evidence

Capture landing and report at 390 and 1440 pixels; check overflow and input visibility across all required sizes. Run scoring/security/payment tests, TypeScript, lint, production build, and Lighthouse where available. Fresh Sol high security/payment review is required. Report missing production credentials and any unverified quality target explicitly.
